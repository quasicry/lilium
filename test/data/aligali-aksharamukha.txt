Mongolian (Ali Gali)
Mongolian (Ali Gali) is an extension to the traditional Mongolian script. It was created in 1587 by the translator and scholar Ayuush Güüsh, inspired by the third Dalai Lama, Sonam Gyatso. He added extra characters for transcribing Tibetan and Sanskrit terms when translating religious texts, and later also from Chinese. (from Wikipedia) See: Omniglot
