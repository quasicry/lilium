uṣṇīṣa vijaya dhāraṇī
oṃ namo bhagavate sarvatrailokya prativiśiṣṭāya buddhāya te namaḥ .
oṃ bhrūṃ bhrūṃ bhrūṃ .
asama samanta avabhāsaspharaṇa gatigagana svabhāva viśuddhe .
abhiṣiñcantu māṃ .
sarvatathāgata sugata varavacana amṛta abhiṣekaiḥ .
mahāmudra mantrapadaiḥ .
mama āyuḥ sandhāraṇi .
uṣṇīṣa vijaya pariśuddhe .
sahasra raśmi saṃcodite .
ṣaṭpāramitā paripūraṇi .
sarvatathāgata mati daśabhūmi pratiṣṭhite .
sarvatathāgata hṛdaya adhiṣṭhāna adhiṣṭhite .
vajrakāya saṃharaṇa pariśuddhe .
sarvakarma āvaraṇa viśuddhe .
sarvatathāgata samaya adhiṣṭhāna adhiṣṭhite .
oṃ muni muni. mahāmuni vimuni vimuni . mahāvimuni .
tathātā bhūtakoṭi pariśuddhe .
visphuṭa buddhe śuddhe .
sarvabuddha adhiṣṭhāna adhiṣṭhite .
vajre udbhave . vajra saṃbhave vajre vajriṇi .
vajraṃ bhavatu mama śarīraṃ sarva sattvānāṃ ca kāya pariśuddhirbhavatu .
me sadā sarvagati pariśuddhiśca . sarvatathāgataśca māṃ samāśvāsayantu .
sarva tathāgata hṛdaya adhiṣṭhāna adhiṣṭhite .
mahāmudrā mantra padaiḥ svāhā ..
oṃ bhrūṃ svāhā .
oṃ amṛta āyurdade svāhā .
